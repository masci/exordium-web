Questions, a problem, something to report: write to [mpippi@gmail.com](mailto:mpippi@gmail.com). We answer as soon as we can.

## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account menu (the person icon), then Delete account. If you signed in with Apple, you confirm with Apple once more. What is erased and what stays is in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

### How do I change my display name?

Account menu, then Change display name. It changes on the standings and on your past submissions too.

### How do I turn notifications off?

Account menu, then Notifications: one switch for new assignments and one for new submissions. If you do not get any, check that notifications are allowed for Exordium in the system settings.

### How do I get a copy of my data?

Write to us. We answer only to the email address of your account. If you signed in with Apple we have no email for you, so we give you a phrase to set as your display name for a moment, to prove that the account is yours; the name of a player or the code of a challenge is not proof. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

### How do I report something a player wrote, or block a player?

In the app, open the menu (the three dots) next to a story, or next to a name on the standings. Choose Report, or Block. Blocking applies from the next assignment, and you can undo it under Account, Blocked players. You can also write to us with the challenge code and what you saw; see the [terms](/terms/).

### I did not get my sign-in code.

Wait a minute, check the spam folder, then ask for a new one in the app. You can also sign in with Apple.
