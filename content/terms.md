By using Exordium you agree to these rules. They are short because the game is meant for people who know each other.

## What you write

- You keep the rights to your stories. By submitting one you let Exordium show it to the other players of that challenge, and keep it in the standings, as the game requires.
- Write only what you are allowed to write. Do not post anything illegal, hateful, threatening or harassing, nothing sexual involving minors, no one’s private information, and nothing that infringes someone else’s rights.
- Do not try to find out who wrote an anonymous story by any means other than playing the game.

## Your account

- You agree to these terms when you create your account in the app. You need to be at least 16 years old.
- Keep your sign-in to yourself. You are responsible for what happens with your account.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time; what stays afterwards is described in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Reporting and removal

If you see something that breaks these rules, write to [mpippi@gmail.com](mailto:mpippi@gmail.com) with the challenge code and what you saw. We read every report and answer within 24 hours. We may remove content or accounts that break the rules: there is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour.

## The service

Exordium is offered as it is, with no promise that it will always be available or free of errors, and we may change it or stop it. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for what players write.

## Changes

If these terms change, the date above changes with them.
